## Behavior

Light and deep maintenance do not depend on a readable/promotable candidate shortlist. After a candidate read fails, their jobs still run and the sweep reports `failed/engine_error`; promotion stays closed. Existing corpus, breaker, idempotency, route and concurrency guards remain in force.

The composition root now reads the public Engine time listing through overlapping inclusive `since`/`until` pages of at most 100 cards, deduplicating actual ids before retaining agent-private cards. This handles corpora larger than 100 across all visible scopes, including ties across a page boundary. **Contract limit:** if more than 100 visible cards occupy one timestamp, the cursor-less API cannot prove completeness. The helper refuses this case instead of losing cards or looping. A future Engine cursor is needed to remove that remaining limit.

Both authoritative statistics and promotion use batches of at most **500 ids**. Every receipt is validated against its own batch and agent. No ledger row advances until all batches validate. A later failure, foreign receipt or cancellation leaves the ledger open, even if an earlier Engine batch wrote knowledge; the next sweep reconciles durable `already-promoted` markers. Missing receipts and genuine skips remain shortlisted.

The fallback field records diagnostic age/repetition/optional-importance evidence only. It changes neither shortlist membership nor promotion eligibility. Production `MemoryCard` has no importance; no score is substituted for it. Actual recall counters (at least three recalls and three distinct queries) remain mandatory. Shared cards and legacy ledger-only ids cannot confer promotion authority.
