# M2 phase 1 — provider selection safety / Produktionsschutz

Status: **partial; not accepted**. Base: `4557176c`. No live calls made.

## Inventory and behavior / Inventar und Verhalten

`composition/embedding-policy.ts` guards `buildEngineConfig` before engine construction. Explicit cloud, unknown, malformed and generic OpenAI-compatible selections fail with existing `E_NOT_AVAILABLE`; explicitly selected self-hosted adapters fail with a separate reason. Previously these selections were silently replaced by local-transformers. Existing local defaults and forced mandatory reranking remain unchanged. No dependency or engine change.

DE: Explizite Provider-Auswahlen werden nicht mehr still durch einen anderen Vektorraum ersetzt. Lokale Standardmodelle funktionieren weiter. Die Server-Adapter sind noch nicht produktiv anschließbar.

## Decisions and engine evidence

The exact pinned engine is `6868b7b117cf7d59b24eb1e75f02aafc1a48f46c`, contract 1.12.0. Its `engine/create-engine.js` constructs either LocalTransformersEmbeddingProvider or OpenAIEmbeddingProvider. `engine/providers/runtime-reranker.js` constructs local-transformers or Cohere. The public `HostServices` contract has no provider factory. `createEngine(..., {internals})` is explicitly **test-only**; using it in production would bypass the public contract and identity handling. Arbitrary provider ids must not enter the engine's generic OpenAI branch.
