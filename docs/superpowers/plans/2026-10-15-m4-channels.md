## Review focus

- Group policy must constrain local commands as well as ordinary messages.
- Provider-owned tool loops must enforce policy as well as TurnRunner-owned loops.
- Restart/crash must preserve encryption state, crypto material and dedupe decisions.
- Revocation and runAs changes must invalidate queued deliveries and links.
- Handoff, pairing, verification and recovery material must never enter logs or ordinary config.
