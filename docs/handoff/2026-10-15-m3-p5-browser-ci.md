## Owner questions, hotspots and known gaps

**KNOWN GAP against a literal all-targets reading of M3 criterion 9:** inline links inside prose retain their normal text line height, which can be below 44/24px. The measurement profile applies only WCAG's semantic Inline exception; it excludes no navigation or standalone control. The inline-prose exception follows WCAG itself. If the owner intends a stricter rule for inline article links, that would require a separate reading-layout decision; navigation and controls meet the numeric minimum here.
